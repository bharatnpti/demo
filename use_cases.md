### UseCase: email_login_issue
#### Description
Customer is unable to log into their email account due to an unknown issue, such as wrong credentials, browser problems, or service unavailability.

#### Steps
Ask the customer if they receive an error message when attempting to log in.

Ask the customer which email provider they are using.

#### Solution
Guide the customer to try the following common solutions:

Clear browser cache and cookies.

Try logging in using incognito or private browsing mode.

Ensure internet connection is stable.

Provide the login help page of their email provider:

Gmail: https://support.google.com/mail/answer/7126229

Yahoo: https://help.yahoo.com/kb/account/login-issues-sln2051.html

Outlook: https://support.microsoft.com/en-us/account-billing/help-with-the-outlook-com-login-page-6c6ea56c-4276-4d10-aed0-06dbe0d00604

#### Alternative Solution
Ask the customer to attempt login from a different device or network (e.g., mobile vs Wi-Fi).

#### Fallback Solution
If the issue persists, escalate to technical support or advise the customer to contact their provider directly for login troubleshooting.

#### Examples
I can’t sign into my Gmail account.

It keeps saying my password is wrong.

Login page doesn’t load for Outlook.

### UseCase: 2fa_locked_out
#### Description
Customer is locked out of their email account because they lost access to their 2-Factor Authentication (2FA) device or app.

#### Steps
Ask the customer if they have any backup methods (e.g., recovery codes, backup phone/email).

Identify the email provider.

#### Solution
Guide the customer to the recovery or 2FA bypass procedure of their email provider:

Gmail: https://support.google.com/accounts/answer/185834

Yahoo: https://help.yahoo.com/kb/SLN5013.html

Microsoft: https://account.live.com/acsr

Apple: https://support.apple.com/en-us/HT204921

#### Alternative Solution
If no recovery method works, advise the customer to submit an identity verification request with the provider.

#### Fallback Solution
If recovery fails after multiple attempts, escalate to a higher tier support team for manual verification.

#### Examples
I can’t access my email because I lost my phone.

My authenticator app isn’t working anymore.

I’m stuck at 2FA verification.

### UseCase: email_not_receiving_reset_link
#### Description
Customer is trying to reset a password on a third-party website, but the password reset email is not arriving in their inbox.

#### Steps
Ask the customer to confirm they entered the correct email address.

Ask them to check their spam/junk folder.

#### Solution
Advise the customer to:

Check spam, junk, or promotions folders.

Add the sender’s domain to their email safe list or contacts.

Wait up to 10–15 minutes, then request the reset link again.

Provide #### Steps for filtering or whitelisting if needed (specific to their provider).

#### Alternative Solution
Suggest trying a different email address (if available) or contact the third-party service’s support directly.

#### Fallback Solution
If the reset email is still not received after multiple attempts, escalate the issue or instruct the customer to contact the website/service directly.

#### Examples
I’m not getting the password reset email.

I clicked “forgot password” but no email came.

It’s not showing up in my inbox or spam.
